It has happened before.

Every time, the people inside said this one was different.

1637Tulip mania

Flowers priced like houses

Bulb contracts in the Netherlands soared, then prices collapsed in a matter of weeks. Nobody wanted a tulip anymore.

1720South Sea Bubble

A company with a story and no profits

British investors, including Isaac Newton, piled into South Sea shares. The crash left Newton bitter about losing a fortune.

1840sRailway mania

Real tech, wrecked investors

Railways did change the world. Plenty of the people who funded them still went broke. Real technology and a bubble can be the same thing.

1929Wall Street crash

Margin, hype and a long fall

The Dow lost roughly 89% from peak to bottom by 1932 and took until 1954 to get back.
